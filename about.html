About Vic’s Bagels

Bethlehem’s neighborhood spot for bagels and breakfast, made fresh and served with a smile.

Forget the rest, try the best

We’ve been serving Bethlehem the way we’d want to be served ourselves: fresh food, made to order, from people who know your name.

Every bagel, sandwich and platter is built the moment you order it.

Bagels done the right way

Soft in the middle, crisp and chewy on the outside. We hand roll and bake all day, so a bagel at noon tastes just as good as one at 5am.

Breakfast, any time you want it

Our egg platters, omelets and made-to-order breakfast sandwiches are built for speed without cutting corners.

Why folks keep coming back

Made from scratch

Bagels, cream cheese and every sandwich are prepared in-house, the same day you eat them.

Fast, friendly counter service

We know breakfast is often a race against the clock, and we move at your pace.

A menu built for everyone

Classic bagel flavors, vegetarian and egg-white options, and plenty in between.
